Details

    Limitations

    • Trained and evaluated on a face-swap/GAN face dataset — not a general "is this AI-generated" detector for arbitrary images.
    • Video is analyzed frame-by-frame with a face classifier, not a dedicated temporal/video model.
    • The temporal jitter score is an experimental heuristic, not a validated detector — camera shake in a real video can also score high.
    • Audio is out of scope.
    • Accuracy is limited to what the classifier saw during training — see README for measured metrics and dataset details.
